Civil Registry (ZPRS) Modulo-23 Algorithm

Citizen National IDs are validated using Zimbabwe's statutory Modulo-23 check character:

  • Format: XX-XXXXXXX-L-XX (District, sequence, checksum letter, origin).
  • Alphabet: Z, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, K, L, M, N, P, Q, R, S, T, V, W, X, Y (excludes I, O, U).
  • Calculation: (DistrictDigits + SequenceDigits) % 23 gives index of check letter.
  • Guarantees zero invalid or fraudulent citizen IDs enter the statutory register.
