The Hard Truth: Why CIPZ Incorporation Is Only Stage 2 of 7

Over 70% of Zimbabwean businesses obtain a Certificate of Incorporation from CIPZ and mistakenly assume they are legally licensed to trade. In statutory reality, CIPZ registration only gives you a legal name and corporate identity. Without ZIMRA Tax Clearance (ITF 263), corporate clients must withhold 30% of your gross invoice. Without Municipal Shop Licensing (Form SL2), city councils can shut down your premises. Without NSSA & PRAZ, you cannot employ workers or tender for contracts.

COBE Act [Chapter 24:31] & S.I. 46 of 2020

Every milestone in this launchpad strictly satisfies Zimbabwe's statutory corporate framework:

  • Section 26: Form CR 2 name search and reservation vetting with CIPZ.
  • Section 112: Form CR 5 mandatory physical registered office in Zimbabwe.
  • Section 195: Form CR 6 requirement of ≥2 directors, with ≥1 ordinarily resident in Zimbabwe.
  • Section 216: Mandatory appointment of a registered company secretary.
  • Section 276: Form CR 16 Beneficial Ownership declaration (≥20% equity or voting control).
Income Tax Act [Chapter 23:06] & ZIMRA

Statutory protection against the 30% withholding penalty:

  • Section 80: Payers must deduct 30% from invoices of unregistered suppliers lacking valid ITF 263.
  • Corporate BP Number: Mandatory linkage of Directors' Mod-23 IDs to Domestic Taxes division.
  • RevMax Fiscalisation: Real-time electronic fiscal device (EFD) registration for VAT compliance.
Cyber & Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]

Complies with POTRAZ Statutory Instrument 155 of 2024 (Data Protection Regulations):

  • Zero Plaintext Storage: Citizen ID numbers and sensitive credentials use authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Blind Indexing: Search queries use HMAC-SHA256 blind indexing to prevent enumeration attacks.
  • Statutory Retention: Vault documents are stored locally and encrypted per Data Controller standards.
Local Municipal & Multi-Agency Clearance

Inter-agency compliance rules for trading premises and labor:

  • Shop Licences Act [Ch 14:17]: Form SL 2 application to Local Authority (e.g. Harare City Council).
  • Urban Councils Act [Ch 29:15]: Commercial & Industrial zoning clearance (Town Planning IND/COM).
  • NSSA Act [Ch 17:04]: Mandatory social security registration within 30 days of hiring first staff.
